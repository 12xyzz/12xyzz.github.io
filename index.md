## About Me
I am a **first-year Ph.D. student at the CVSSP, University of Surrey**, supervised by [Dr. Xiatian Zhu](https://x-up-lab.github.io/). Previously, I received my Master's degree from National University of Singapore, and my Bachelor's degree from Northwestern Polytechnical University.  

✅ I am actively seeking **research intern / visiting student** opportunities.

## Research
My research explores generative *world models* that go beyond visual prediction to learn structured representations of the physical world, capturing how objects, actions, and environments evolve across space and time.

Alongside this, I study AI for cryptography, exploring how learning-based methods can support cryptanalysis and clarify its implications for computational security.
